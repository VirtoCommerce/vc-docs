# View Products Snapshots

The product snapshot feature is valuable anywhere you need a historically accurate representation of what the customer actually purchased at the moment of the order:

* Product substitution or misleading changes after purchase. 
* Price and promotion dispute investigations.
* Product deletion from the catalog. 
* Returns and warranty claims.
* Historical analytics and business intelligence.
* Customer support and order troubleshooting.
* Catalog migrations and replatforming.

In all the above cases, the snapshot preserves the original state shown to the customer during checkout and helps resolve disputes.
