# Settings

Within the **Pages** module, you can configure:

* [Global settings.](#global-settings)
* [Store-specific settings.](#store-specific-settings)

## Global settings

To configure the module settings globally:

1. Click **Settings** in the main menu.
1. In the search field of the next blade, type **Pages** to find the settings related to the module.
1. In the next blade,  configure the following:

    ![Global settings](media/settings.png)

1. Click **Save** in the toolbar to save the changes.

Your modifications have been applied.

<br>
<br>

## Store-specific settings

To configure the store-specific module settings:

1. Open **Stores** from the main menu.
1. In the next blade, select  your store.
1. In the next blade, click on the **Settings** widget.
1. Type **Pages** in the search bar and select it from the list.
1. In the next blade,  enable or disable the Pages module for the store.
1. Click **OK**, then **Save** in the toolbar to save the changes.

Your modifications have been applied.
